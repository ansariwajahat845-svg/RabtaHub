📜 RabtaHub Rules

← Back

1. Respect Everyone

Har user ke saath izzat aur achay andaaz mein baat karein.

2. No Harassment

Kisi user ko tang, threaten ya repeatedly disturb na karein.

3. No Spam

Repeated unwanted messages, fake promotions ya spam allowed nahi.

4. Privacy

Kisi doosre user ki personal information bina permission share na karein.

5. Appropriate Content

Platform ko safe aur respectful rakhne ke liye inappropriate ya harmful content post na karein.

6. Fake Accounts

Dusre user ki identity copy karna ya impersonation allowed nahi.

7. Report & Block

Agar koi user rules violate kare to usay block/report karein.

8. Admin Decision

Serious rule violations par administration zaroori action le sakti hai.
